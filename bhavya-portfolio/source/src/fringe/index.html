      The patch test

      Colour and lash tint can cause a reaction. We dab a little behind your ear at least 48 hours before your appointment and check it when you arrive. It takes five minutes, costs nothing, and you can drop in any day we're open.

      Had one with us in the last six months? You can skip it.

      Changes and lateness

      Change or cancel for free up to 24 hours before. If you're more than 15 minutes late we may need to shorten the service so the next client isn't kept waiting.

      Can I book brows and a haircut together?

      Brows and lashes happen in a separate room with Tenzin, so they're booked as their own appointment. Book the hair first, then the brows.

      Do you take a deposit?

      Not on this demo site, and no payment is ever taken here. A live version could take a small deposit for long colour services.

      What if I need more than three services?

      Book the three that matter most and add a note. The stylist will see what fits on the day.
