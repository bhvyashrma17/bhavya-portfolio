How we brew

Filter kaapi starts the night before, with a blend of 80% coffee and 20% chicory from estates in Chikmagalur. Here is what happens to your cup.

  1. Pack the filter

    28 grams of the blend go into the top chamber of a brass-and-steel filter, pressed down lightly with the plunger.

  2. Let it drip

    Hot water drips through for about twenty minutes and collects as a thick decoction in the lower chamber.

  3. Pour it high

    Decoction and hot milk are poured between tumbler and dabara from about 30 cm, until the top turns to froth.
