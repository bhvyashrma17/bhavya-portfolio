ClearTrade

An export operating system for Indian businesses. Exporters find markets and buyers for free, and when they are ready to ship, a ClearTrade agent handles the paperwork, customs, freight and payment with them.

Visit cleartrade.in (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Exporting means juggling buyer research, documents, customs, freight and payment across email, spreadsheets and agents. Smaller exporters often can't see which countries already buy their product, or what a deal involves before they start.

What is live

  • Market search by HS code, built on customs-recorded trade history, free and without an account
  • Buyer discovery and a saved lead list for free accounts
  • An eight-stage deal pipeline, from readiness to payment, that an agent works through with the exporter
  • A paid plan with a one-time commitment fee for agent-led deals
  • Sample results for markets, buyers and shipment documents, clearly marked as samples

My role

Design and development. ClearTrade is my own product and is live at cleartrade.in.

Built with

Live web product with free search, accounts and a paid plan.

Mettle Strength Club

A website for a fictional strength club, built around the two things gym visitors look for: what they would do there, and what it costs.

View live project (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Many gym sites hide the timetable in a PDF and the prices behind "contact us", so visitors leave before they ask. This concept puts programmes, class times and an honest price calculator on the first visit.

What I built

  • Programme browser with search and filters for goal, experience and format
  • Programme sheets with who it is for, a typical week, a sample session and class times
  • Weekly timetable with day tabs on phones and per-programme filtering
  • Membership builder that prices coaching, days, off-peak, commitment and extras as you choose, including GST
  • Trial and membership request forms with validation, clearly marked as demos that send nothing
  • A working rest timer in the opening section, because it is a gym

My role

Concept, design, copy and front-end development.

Built with

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, no framework. Responsive, keyboard accessible and respects reduced motion.

Independent project, concept demo

Fringe Hair Studio

An appointment-booking flow for a fictional hair, colour and brow studio, from choosing services to a confirmation.

View live project (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Booking a salon by phone or DM means back-and-forth about free times, prices that change by stylist, and colour clients who forget the patch test.

What I built

  • Service picker for up to three services, with durations and running totals
  • Stylist choice with level-based pricing, or "any available"
  • Calendar and start times worked out from opening hours, each stylist's day off and existing appointments
  • The 48-hour patch-test rule for colour and lash tints, explained wherever it blocks a date
  • An appointment-book view that draws your slot among the day's bookings
  • Review, a demo confirmation with copyable details, and "My bookings" to change the time or cancel

My role

Concept, interaction design, booking logic and front-end development.

Built with

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. Availability is generated per stylist and day, and demo bookings stay in the browser.

Dabara Coffee House

A menu and pickup-ordering website for a fictional South Indian coffee bar.

View live project (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Café menus online are often a photo or a PDF: hard to read on a phone, impossible to filter, and no way to order ahead.

What I built

  • A 26-item menu with search, category chips and sorting
  • Dietary filters for no egg, vegan, gluten-free and nut-free, with veg and egg marks
  • Item options that change the price, such as oat milk
  • A basket drawer with quantities, pickup times that follow opening hours, and GST
  • A demo order confirmation that says plainly nothing was sent or paid
  • Illustrations drawn for every item, and a basket that is kept between visits

My role

Concept, illustration, design and front-end development.

Built with

HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with hand-drawn SVG illustrations.

Hookloop Social Studio

A social design and motion showcase for a fictional content studio, with four campaigns for fictional brands.

View live project (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Social design is judged at phone size and in motion, so flat mockups undersell it. This showcase presents finished pieces at their real sizes, and motion that plays.

What I built

  • 20 original graphics: feed posts, carousels, stories, reel covers, squares and YouTube thumbnails
  • Campaigns for Mettle and Dabara (the demos above), a bookshop and a cooking channel
  • A gallery with brand and format filters, a lightbox with keyboard and swipe, and a safe-zone overlay
  • Three motion pieces rendered frame by frame at 30 fps, with custom players and timecode

My role

Art direction, graphic design, motion design and front-end development.

Built with

Graphics built in HTML, CSS and SVG and rendered at exact pixel sizes; motion encoded as H.264 video. No stock images.

How a project runs

Four steps, with something real to look at from the second one.

  1. A short call

    We talk through what the business needs and what a good result looks like. I send a written brief back.

  2. Design

    You see real pages in the browser early, on your own phone, and we adjust them together.

  3. Build

    I build and test on phones and desktops, and connect the forms and tools the business needs.

  4. Launch

    I put the site live, show you how updates work, and hand over everything you paid for.
