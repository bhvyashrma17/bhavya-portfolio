A website for Only Hands, a 24-hour café on Gandhi Road in Vrindavan. It shows what the place feels like, what to order, what people say about it and how to get there.

Visit the live site (opens in a new tab)

What is on the site

  • A comfort-menu section with tabs for street food, café comforts and sips, and a full menu that expands in place
  • Links out to the café's Zomato and Swiggy listings for current prices
  • The Google rating and selected review excerpts, linking to the originals
  • The 24/7 promise, directions, a call button and the café's Instagram handle
  • A phone-first layout with its own menu, built around the café's own photos

My role

My product, in progress

ClearTrade

An export operating system for Indian businesses. Exporters find markets and buyers for free, and when they are ready to ship, a ClearTrade agent handles the paperwork, customs, freight and payment with them.

Visit cleartrade.in (opens in a new tab)

The problem

Exporting means juggling buyer research, documents, customs, freight and payment across email, spreadsheets and agents. Smaller exporters often can't see which countries already buy their product.

What is live

  • Market search by HS code, built on customs-recorded trade history, free and without an account
  • Buyer discovery and a saved lead list for free accounts
  • An eight-stage deal pipeline, from readiness to payment, worked through with an agent
  • A paid plan with a one-time commitment fee for agent-led deals
  • Sample results for markets, buyers and documents, clearly marked as samples

My role

Design and development. ClearTrade is my own product.

Built with

Live web product with free search, accounts and a paid plan.

Mettle Strength Club

A website for a fictional strength club, built around what gym visitors look for first: what they would do there, and what it costs.

Open the demo

The problem

Many gym sites hide the timetable in a PDF and the prices behind "contact us", so visitors leave before they ask. This puts programmes, class times and an honest price calculator on the first visit.

What I built

  • Programme finder with search and filters for goal, experience and format
  • Programme sheets with who it is for, a typical week, a sample session and class times
  • Weekly timetable with day tabs on phones
  • Membership builder that prices coaching, days, off-peak, commitment and extras live, including GST
  • Trial and membership request forms, clearly marked as demos that send nothing
  • A working rest timer in the opening section

My role

Concept, design, copy and front-end development.

Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Responsive and keyboard accessible.

Fringe Hair Studio

An appointment-booking flow for a fictional hair, colour and brow studio, from choosing services to a confirmation.

Open the demo

The problem

Booking a salon by phone or DM means back-and-forth about free times, prices that change by stylist, and colour clients who forget the patch test.

What I built

  • Service picker for up to three services, with durations and running totals
  • Stylist choice with level-based pricing, or "any available"
  • Calendar and start times worked out from opening hours, days off and existing appointments
  • The 48-hour patch-test rule, explained wherever it blocks a date
  • An appointment-book view that draws your slot among the day's bookings
  • Review, a demo confirmation, and "My bookings" to change the time or cancel

My role

Concept, interaction design, booking logic and front-end development.

Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Demo bookings stay in the browser.

Dabara Coffee House

A menu and pickup-ordering website for a fictional South Indian coffee bar.

Open the demo

The problem

Café menus online are often a photo or a PDF: hard to read on a phone, impossible to filter, and no way to order ahead.

What I built

  • A 26-item menu with search, category chips and sorting
  • Dietary filters for no egg, vegan, gluten-free and nut-free
  • Item options that change the price, such as oat milk
  • A basket with quantities, pickup times that follow opening hours, and GST
  • A demo order confirmation that says plainly nothing was sent or paid
  • Illustrations drawn for every item

My role

Concept, illustration, design and front-end development.

Built with

HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with hand-drawn SVG illustrations.

Hookloop Social Studio

A social design and motion showcase for a fictional content studio, with campaigns for four fictional brands.

Open the showcase

The problem

Social design is judged at phone size and in motion, so flat mockups undersell it. This shows finished pieces at their real sizes, and motion that plays.

What I built

  • 20 original graphics: feed posts, carousels, stories, reel covers, squares and YouTube thumbnails
  • A gym campaign (programme launch and a new over-50s group), a café launch, a bookshop and a cooking channel
  • A gallery with brand and format filters, a lightbox with swipe, and a safe-zone overlay
  • Three motion pieces rendered at 30 fps, with custom players and timecode

My role

Art direction, graphic design, motion design and front-end development.

Built with

Graphics built in HTML, CSS and SVG and rendered at exact sizes; motion encoded as H.264 video.
