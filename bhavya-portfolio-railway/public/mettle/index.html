What does “RPE 8” mean?

Rate of perceived exertion. An 8 means you finish the set with about two good reps left. Coaches use it to set the weight for the day you are actually having.

      Questions

      I've never lifted a barbell. Is that a problem?

      No. Strength Foundations exists for exactly that. You'll start with kettlebells and dumbbells, and move to the barbell when your coach is happy with your technique.

      What should I bring?

      Flat shoes or trainers, a water bottle and clothes you can squat in. Chalk, belts and towels are provided at the club.

      Can I pause my membership?

      Six and twelve-month memberships can be frozen for up to four weeks a year, for travel or illness.

      Do you have showers and lockers?

      Yes, both. Locker and towel service is an optional extra if you want a permanent locker.

      Can I switch programmes mid-block?

      You can, but most people finish the six weeks first so the re-test means something. Talk to your coach either way.
