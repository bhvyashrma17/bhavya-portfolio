## Excluded: learning is free but the credential costs money (or needs a paid trial)

- **Course platforms:**
  - Coursera professional certificates from Meta and Google, which need a subscription;
  - edX (no certificate on the free track);
  - Alison (paid certificates);
  - Great Learning Academy (certificate fee);
  - NPTEL (exam fee).
- **Coding sites:** W3Schools ($95 exam), Codecademy (Pro only), Uxcel (paid plans).
- **Platform certifications:**
  - Meta Blueprint certifications ($99–150 exams);
  - Hootsuite Academy ($199+);
  - Webflow certification ($100 per attempt);
  - Adobe Certified Professional ($150 exam);
  - Shopify Verified Skills ($99+).
